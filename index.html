How to play

First select a territory and commander, then place your commander on a glowing landscape. Your commander determines your 56-card battle deck and passive. Choose a battle card, then tap a highlighted landscape. Each turn grants 2 AP. Each lane holds one creature and one building. Fight attacks all opposing lanes: creatures exchange damage; empty lanes expose the rival commander. Floop uses your creature’s kingdom ability instead of attacking. The first player cannot fight or Floop on turn one. Frozen creatures miss their next turn. Reduce the rival from 30 HP to zero. Empty decks inflict increasing fatigue damage: 2, then 4, then 6 HP, and so on.

Tap any creature or building on the table to inspect its full card. Cards reshape the isometric field: inspect terrain, food, and field integrity under each lane. Fields at 0 integrity lose their buildings and take +1 direct attack damage. Thorn fields damage their creatures each turn. Animals move and forage in real time; food and combat bonuses resolve at turn end so waiting gives no extra battle rewards. Crow Barrage hits up to three enemy creatures, or damages your own field when none exist.
