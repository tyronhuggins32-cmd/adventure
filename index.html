How to play

Play the episode table for Blue Plains versus Corn Kingdom, or choose a commander deck below it. Play cards onto one of four landscapes using 3 AP per turn. Creatures can attack, Floop, shelter or study; using an ability replaces that creature’s attack. Reveal the rival turn, make your Floop or building response, then resolve the world battle. Pause and speed controls affect the whole simulation.

Floop the Pig to queue a raid across the rival cornfields. The raid happens before attacks: eaten cornfields lose their crops and stop powering Husker Knights and the Maize Walker. A mud landscape traps the pig, while Earlings frighten it. Reclaim Landscape restores your whole kingdom and recalls its trapped pig.

The Schoolhouse teaches revival after two surviving study turns. The Cave of Solitude shelters its creature behind the building’s health. Floop the Silo to reveal and steal from the rival hand, the Spirit Tower to capture a spirit, or the Volcano to burn your own kingdom and strike an opposing building. Arche​r Dan fires at buildings. Blood Storm harms attacking creatures. Field Reaper possesses a creature for one battle. Teleport moves only your own creatures.

Food, hunger and morale persist between battles. Fire can spread into corn; rain extinguishes it. These simulation rules, the AP budget and unspecified card statistics are additions to the episode’s demonstrated interactions.
