STEP 1 · LANDSCAPE DECKBuild your side of the table
The field is your first deck. Pick four landscape cards; each one becomes a real tile.

Build the field. Play the cards. Watch the fight.

Choose four landscape cards and tap the numbered slots or the table to lay them down. A selected field can replace a slot before you start. Press START MATCH to deal the battle hand.

During planning, select a creature, building, or spell and tap a landscape. Cards land and become raised miniatures. Battle runs as a live simulation: figures choose where to go, find food, flee, fight, and damage buildings. The next planning turn begins after 22 seconds.

With no card selected, tap a lane to open its full-screen view. Use the arrows to switch lanes and × to return. On the whole table, drag to orbit and tilt. In a lane, drag to travel along it, use Orbit or Alt-drag to turn, and pinch or use +/− to zoom. Select a creature during planning, then press Floop for its ability.
