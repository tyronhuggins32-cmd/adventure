How this table lives

Pick a card in your hand, then tap one of the four landscapes or its numbered marker. Creatures, buildings and spells cost energy. Press Battle to let the simulation run; you can pause or speed it up. The next planning turn begins after 22 seconds.

Pigs forage for corn and edible creatures by themselves. Corn powers Husker Knights. A Barn births sheep and adds Cotton Volley. Scarecrows repel crows, and Snow Wolves strengthen nearby packmates. Buildings and damaged creatures persist between turns.

Tap one of your figures during planning and use Floop for its ability. The board can orbit, pan and zoom. Defeat Finn by bringing his health to zero.
