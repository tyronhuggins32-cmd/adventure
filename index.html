Choose your Kingdom Deck

Your commander creates four matching landscapes. Each deck includes creatures, buildings, and spells.

Your hand

How to play

Choose a card, then tap one of your four landscapes. You receive 2 action points each turn. Each lane holds one creature and one building. Fight attacks all opposing lanes: creatures exchange damage; empty lanes expose the enemy hero. Floop activates a creature’s kingdom power but uses its attack for the turn. The starting player cannot fight or Floop on turn one. Units frozen by an enemy miss their next turn. Reduce the rival hero from 30 HP to zero. Empty decks cause 2 fatigue damage whenever you draw.
