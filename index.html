    How the world works

    Plan while paused, then run the simulation. Creatures keep their positions and decisions between pauses. Melee requires contact; arrows deal damage on arrival. Corn powers Husker Knights, pigs eat crops and food creatures, mud traps pigs, fire spreads through corn, and rain extinguishes it.

    Select a friendly creature for orders. Move orders hold the chosen ground; Auto restores its own decisions. Barns and caves shelter frightened or injured creatures. Study at a Schoolhouse unlocks resurrection after twenty seconds inside. Buildings unlock their own recruits. Cards spend energy; your kingdom regains one energy every four seconds.

    The shared world measures 288 × 288. The barn unlocks Cotton Siege when sheep arrive; a living scarecrow teaches Corn Maze. Snow wolves gain strength by staying together. Drag, orbit, tilt and zoom to explore it. These are expanded simulation rules inspired by the tabletop concept.
